Sources and definitions

BD uploaded 10-K: PDF pp.35, 37, 68, 70–73. Baxter: pp.33, 60–61, 64, 68. Medtronic uploaded 10-K (FY2026): pp.31, 58, 60, 62, 66, 113. PDF page positions, not printed numbers.

  • DIO = average opening/closing net inventory / reported annual COGS × 365.
  • Trade working capital = closing receivables + inventory − accounts payable.
  • FCF = continuing operating cash flow − capital expenditure. Baxter uses continuing OCF $951m, not total OCF $845m.
  • Medtronic COGS excludes intangible amortization; BD includes acquisition effects. Provisions and unusual costs affect DIO. No accounting normalization is fabricated.
  • Manufacturing/research area, all-use facility counts and principal plant lists are different categories. Do not compare them as plant counts.
  • BD FY2025 includes Life Sciences. New structure from October 2025: Medical Essentials, Connected Care, BioPharma Systems, Interventional and transitional Life Sciences. The separation presentation is context, not a plant register.
  • No division-level inventory or cash flow is allocated without disclosure.
