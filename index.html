How to read this tab. Opportunities are hypotheses generated from public benchmarks and BD's stated priorities (10-K: BD Excellence factory productivity, inventory optimization, simplification, manufacturing capacity expansion). Sizes are sensitivities on BD FY2025 figures, not forecasts or commitments. Peer portfolios, accounting and reporting periods differ, so each lever needs an internal baseline before any target is set. One-time cash and annual profit are shown separately and must not be added together.

Where BD stands

What the benchmark does not show

  • It does not show that BD underperforms. On working capital BD leads this peer set.
  • It does not provide plant-level or division-level inventory, so lever sizes are consolidated sensitivities.
  • Distribution cost is only the disclosed shipping line, not warehousing, 3PL, duties or tariffs.
  • Medtronic is a different business model and scale; use it as a directional reference, not a target.

Definitions

  • DIO uses average opening/closing net inventory and reported COGS. DSO and DPO on the opportunities tab use closing balances; the cash conversion cycle mixes these bases and is approximate.
  • Trade working capital = closing receivables + inventory − accounts payable.
  • FCF = continuing operating cash flow − capex. Baxter continuing OCF $951m is used, not total $845m.
  • Medtronic COGS excludes intangible amortization; BD includes acquisition-related items. No normalization applied.
  • Shipping is a narrow 10-K disclosure, not total logistics cost.

Footprint disclosure

  • Baxter 10-K Item 2 lists principal plants by segment (36 entries; St. Paul appears twice). Coordinates are approximate city locations.
  • Medtronic discloses 10.2m sq ft of manufacturing and research space (41% owned) and its largest manufacturing countries; many sites serve several divisions. Markers are country-level.
  • BD reports 290 owned/leased facilities, 26.1m sq ft, all uses. No plant-to-division list; markers are plants named in BD public announcements, incomplete and partly dated.
